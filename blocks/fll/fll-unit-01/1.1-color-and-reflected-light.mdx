# Lesson F1.1: Color and Reflected Light

The color sensor can classify colors or report reflected-light intensity. Reflected light is often better for finding the edge of a dark line because the program can compare a number with a tested threshold.

Mount height, room light, surface wear, and sensor angle change the reading. Measure the light and dark surfaces on the real mat, then choose a threshold between those readings.

## Official hardware activity

Complete [Training Camp 3: Reacting to Lines](https://education.lego.com/en-us/lessons/prime-competition-ready/training-camp-3-reacting-to-lines/) with the official practice base.

## Simulator practice

Use `move until reflected light is below 35%`. Run from home and watch the reading fall when the sensor reaches the black line.
