# Lesson F1.4: Attachment Motors and Objects

A mission tool can push, lift, pull, or hold an object. Its motor command must match the mechanism's safe range. Telemark models one front arm from 0 degrees open to 90 degrees closed.

Sequence matters. Approach first, close or move the tool, confirm the object is controlled, drive to the destination, and release. On a physical robot, test for stalls and avoid forcing a mechanism against a hard stop.

## Official hardware activity

Use [Time for an Upgrade](https://education.lego.com/en-us/lessons/prime-competition-ready/time-for-an-upgrade/) to build and evaluate a motorized competition tool.

## Simulator practice

Move near the red object, close the attachment above 55 degrees, drive toward the green zone, then open below 12 degrees.
