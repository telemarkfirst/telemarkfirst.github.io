# Lesson F1.2: Distance and Wait Until

The distance sensor estimates how far the nearest visible surface is from the sensor. A wait-until approach keeps moving while the reading is above a threshold, then stops close to the object.

This is a stopping rule, so it needs a safe limit. A sensor can miss a narrow, angled, transparent, or low object. Telemark limits the search distance; a physical program should also include a maximum travel or time.

## Official hardware activity

Complete [Training Camp 2: Playing with Objects](https://education.lego.com/en-us/lessons/prime-competition-ready/training-camp-2-playing-with-objects/).

## Simulator practice

Turn toward the loose object or target, then use a distance stop. Compare a 5 cm threshold with a 20 cm threshold.
