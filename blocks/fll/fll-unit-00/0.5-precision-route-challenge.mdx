# Lesson F0.5: Coding Challenge: Precision Route

Leave the blue home region, cross the dark line, and finish beyond x = 40 cm. Use at least one drive block and either a turn or steering block. The robot must avoid the center obstacle and every field wall.

Plan the route on paper before building it. Use the trail and pose readout to find the first command that needs correction.
