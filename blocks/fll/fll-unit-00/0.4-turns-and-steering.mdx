# Lesson F0.4: Point, Pivot, and Arc Turns

A point turn drives the wheels in opposite directions and changes heading in a small space. A pivot turn holds one wheel while the other moves. An arc turn moves both sides at different speeds and changes position while turning.

The turn block models a point turn by angle. The steer block models an arc: zero is straight, a positive or negative value bends the path, and a larger magnitude bends it more sharply. Real turn angles depend on wheel spacing, tire grip, and weight distribution.

## Official hardware activity

Continue the turning experiments in [Training Camp 1](https://education.lego.com/en-us/lessons/prime-competition-ready/training-camp-1-driving-around/). Mark the starting wheel positions so point and pivot turns can be compared.

## Simulator practice

Build a 90 degree point turn followed by a straight drive. Replace both blocks with one steering move and compare the trail.
