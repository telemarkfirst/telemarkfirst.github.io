# Lesson F2.2: Launch Alignment, Route Plans, and Home

A program assumes a starting pose. Use flat robot surfaces against consistent guides in home, select the intended program, and make the launch procedure repeatable. A few degrees of starting error becomes a large sideways error after a long drive.

Plan the return before the outbound route. Returning home can let the team change an attachment or choose another program, subject to the current Robot Game rules.

## Official hardware activity

Work through [The Guided Mission](https://education.lego.com/en-us/lessons/prime-competition-ready/the-guided-mission/) and compare its launch setup with your team's field.

## Simulator practice

Cross the line or activate the target, then reverse or turn back toward the blue home region. End with x below -82 cm and z above 25 cm.
