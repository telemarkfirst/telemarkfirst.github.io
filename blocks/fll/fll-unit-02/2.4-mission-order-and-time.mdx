# Lesson F2.4: Mission Order, Recovery, and Time

Mission order is a route-planning problem. Combine nearby objectives when the added attachment and alignment risk is small. Split a long route when returning home makes the next launch simpler and more reliable.

The simulator estimates motion and attachment time and stops programs beyond 150 seconds. A real match also spends time on launches, attachment changes, and recovery. Leave margin rather than planning to exactly 150 seconds.

## Official hardware activity

Use [Mission Training: Creative Problem-Solving](https://education.lego.com/en-us/lessons/prime-competition-ready/mission-training-creative-problem-solving/) to compare route and attachment strategies.

## Simulator practice

Activate the yellow target and cross the line in either order. Compare elapsed time and path length, then add a return home.
